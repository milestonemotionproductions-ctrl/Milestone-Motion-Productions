Questions and answers

What kinds of animation do you make?

Logo animations, animated social media posts, explainers, titles and ads, plus video edits. The FARAS Cabs page shows 18 examples.

Which software do you use for animation?

Adobe After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Blender, with Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop for artwork, and OpenToonz and Krita for hand-drawn animation.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. I work with clients across East Africa, including Dar es Salaam, Dodoma, Morogoro, Kampala and Kigali, by WhatsApp, email and video calls.
