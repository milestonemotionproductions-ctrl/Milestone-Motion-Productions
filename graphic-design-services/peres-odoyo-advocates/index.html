Case study · Legal services

Peres Odoyo & Co. Advocates

A full brand kit for a Kisumu law firm and commissioner for oaths: logo, desk calendar, roll-up banners, embroidered polos and festive cards.

  • Logo
  • Calendar
  • Roll-up banners
  • Apparel
  • Greeting cards

The laurel wreath, scales and pillar of the logo carry through everything, in deep blue, gold and copper tones. The kit includes a 2021 desk calendar, roll-up banners with the firm’s contact details, embroidered polo shirts in two colour options, and Merry Christmas and Happy New Year cards.
