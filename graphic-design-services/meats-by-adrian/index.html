Meats By Adrian

A logo with packaging, a cap and an apron for a meat business.

  • Logo
  • Packaging

The logo combines a cleaver, a chef’s hat, a steak and a spatula, with the name set between two stars. It is shown on food trays with printed wraps, on a two-tone baseball cap and on a white apron with yellow straps.
