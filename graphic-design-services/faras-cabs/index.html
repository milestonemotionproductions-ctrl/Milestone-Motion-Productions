Case study · Transport & mobility

FARAS

A series of social media posts, a print flyer and campaign visuals for FARAS, a ride-hailing company in Kenya.

  • Social media
  • Print flyer
  • Campaign design
  • Promotions

Discounts, driver recruitment, corporate rides and a road-safety message each get their own layout, all kept recognisably FARAS green. The set covers the Every Match, Every Goal, Every Ride promo, the Move Stress Free and Captain recruitment posts, the Ride with Standards corporate post, a Stay Safe post for International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction, and a Brand with FARAS and Earn flyer.
