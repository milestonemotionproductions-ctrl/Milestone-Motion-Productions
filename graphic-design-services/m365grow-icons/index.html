M365Grow

A four-icon set for M365Grow, taken from paper sketches to finished vector icons.

  • Icon design
  • Illustration
  • Adobe Illustrator

Each icon carries a meaning. Leaves and a bulb stand for growth and energy in education. Hands, a heart and a dollar sign stand for community, partnerships and donations. A certificate and a globe stand for global education, and a padlock with wifi and a tick stands for connection and access.

The whole project took about four hours, from research through design to compiling the sheets. It was all done in Adobe Illustrator, with the text set in the Register typeface.
