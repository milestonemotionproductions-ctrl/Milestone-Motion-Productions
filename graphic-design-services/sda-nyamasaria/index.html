Seventh-day Adventist Church, Nyamasaria

Event artwork for a church in Nyamasaria, Kisumu: a baby dedication banner and card, and Pathfinder induction Sabbath invitations.

  • Banner
  • Greeting card
  • Invitations
  • Church

A tall pink banner for the child dedication service, and a matching card with a short blessing, floral border and a red ribbon, built around a black-and-white photo of a baby’s hand in a parent’s hand.

The Pathfinder induction Sabbath invitation, for 8 March 2025 on the church grounds, uses layered white panels, the Pathfinder emblem and the church logo. A second version adds a registration fee of Ksh 150 per child.
