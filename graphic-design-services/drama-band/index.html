Drama Band

Posters and brand identity for Drama Band’s stage events in Nairobi.

  • Event posters
  • Logo
  • Ticketing
  • Print and social
  • T-shirts

Posters for three productions: A Community Transformed on 22 March 2025, Your Heart, His Light on 27 June 2025, and We Judge But We Don’t Listen on 27 July 2025, with tickets at KSH 750. The logo also appears on T-shirts in four colourways.

We Judge But We Don’t Listen came as four poster concepts for the same production, from a dark rose portrait to a Nairobi skyline collage. A Community Transformed has four poster versions: red and white, blue and orange, black and orange, and a bright yellow one.
