Questions and answers

What does a 3D character project include?

It can include modeling and digital sculpting, texturing, rigging so the character can move, and animation or still renders. We agree the steps you need at the start.

Which software do you use for 3D work?

Blender, Substance 3D Painter and Marvelous Designer for modeling, texturing and clothing, with Adobe After Effects and DaVinci Resolve for motion and editing.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. I work with clients across East Africa, including Dar es Salaam, Dodoma, Morogoro, Kampala and Kigali, by WhatsApp, email and video calls.

How do I start a project?
